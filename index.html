Made with p5.js and vanilla ES modules; no frameworks, no build step. Motifs are rasterised to integer loom cells and coloured with natural-dye values — madder, indigo, walnut, henna — then aged. All chance runs through one seeded generator, so every specimen carries a citable seed and can be re-woven exactly.

Motif grids, meanings and regional catalog follow the project's research reference; variants are auto-traced from Mine Erbek, Çatalhöyük'ten Günümüze Anadolu Motifleri (T.C. Kültür Bakanlığı) and are marked pending validation until checked against the printed page. Nothing here is invented: every motif comes from the documented catalog.

A woven kilim may seem to carry a mistake — a motif a cell off, a colour that changes order mid-band, a hook that does not close. This is deliberate. Kilims carry their authenticity through such irregularities, the trace of a hand rather than a machine, and the library keeps them as an aesthetic choice.
